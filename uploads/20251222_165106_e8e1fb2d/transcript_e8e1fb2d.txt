 Classical conditioning involves learning through association between two stimuli, whereas operant conditioning involves learning through consequences of behavior. Cognitive dissonance is the psychological discomfort that arises when a person holds conflict in beliefs when their behavior conflicts with their beliefs. For example, somebody who values health but smokes, they resolve this discomfort by quitting smoking or by downplaying the health risks. Confirmation bias is the tendency to seek out interpret and remember information that supports ones existing beliefs while ignoring contradictory evidence. In everyday decision making, this can lead people to make poor choices, such as only reading news that aligns with their political views, becoming more confident and inaccurate beliefs.
